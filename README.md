## 🍓 Featured Projects

### 01. PetCareOps 🐾

**Pet Grooming Management System**

A web-based application designed to manage pet grooming operations, appointments, staff scheduling, and customer information.

**Key Focus:**
- Role-based authentication and authorization
- Staff and user management
- Database design and relational data management
- Grooming services and appointment workflows
- Backend business logic and scheduling

`Laravel 12` `PHP` `PostgreSQL` `Supabase` `Spatie Permission`

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/Adeliaswa/petcareops)

### 02. WBS Donasi Masjid 🕌

**Web-Based Mosque Donation Management System**

A web application developed to support mosque donation management, including donation records, beneficiary data, and financial transparency.

**Key Focus:**
- Authentication and role-based access
- Donation management (CRUD)
- Beneficiary management
- Database integration
- Donation verification and reporting

`Laravel 12` `PHP` `PostgreSQL` `Supabase` `JavaScript`

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/Adeliaswa/WBS-Donasi-Masjid)
